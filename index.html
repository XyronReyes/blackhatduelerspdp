Notes expanded from Blackhat's published flavor descriptors for this expression. Final copy should be reviewed against the master tasting panel before publishing.

Duelers began as an attempt to build something new between two traditions — not a tequila, and not a whiskey, but a spirit disciplined by both. Every batch starts with 100% Blue Weber agave, grown in the Highlands of Jalisco by a farmer-owned producer, then cut with pure Rocky Mountain water back home in Colorado Springs.

Fermentation with a specialty yeast strain builds the line's signature soft, sweet-and-spiced palate, and clean, precise distillation cuts keep the spirit's character intact through to the barrel. For Double Oaked, that spirit spends extra time resting on a second cask — French oak — adding the depth and structure that give this expression its name. That extra time on French oak specifically is what sets Double Oaked apart from the rest of the line.

Duelers is the start of something truly different in the world of spirits. — Blackhat Distillery, on the Duelers line
  • 100% Blue Weber AgaveSourced from a farmer-owned producer in the Highlands of Jalisco.
  • Rocky Mountain WaterCut to proof with pure water sourced near the Colorado Springs distillery.
  • Double-Oak FinishRested a second time on French oak.
  • Small, Disciplined BatchesDistilled and bottled in Colorado Springs, Colorado.
